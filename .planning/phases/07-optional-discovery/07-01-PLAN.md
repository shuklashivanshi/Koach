<objective>
Make the committed optional data trustworthy before any screen shows it.

Purpose: OPT-05 says official annual-report counts drive the success rates. Today the stats rows are joined to the profiles by exact slug, and the Political Science profile slug is truncated to 40 characters by the syllabus generator while its 6 years of official rows use the full slug. The join silently drops all of it, so one of the most-taken optionals shows "No official data" and gets a neutral stability score. Nothing in the repo would have caught it.

Output: a canonical-slug join, a real-data integrity and ranking test, and a soft-flag audit script.

What already exists (do not redo): `optional_info` table and `learner_profiles` optional columns (migration 0008), `packages/kb/src/optionals.ts` fit maths (FIT_WEIGHTS, wilson, pooledSuccess, scoreOptional, rankOptionals) with 2 unit tests, `packages/kb/data/optionals/profiles.json` (48 profiles), `packages/kb/data/optionals/stats.json` (6 reports: exam years 2013, 2016, 2017, 2020, 2021, 2022; sources are the 6 English annual reports named in D-01), generator scripts `optional-profiles.ts` and `optional-stats.ts`.
</objective>
