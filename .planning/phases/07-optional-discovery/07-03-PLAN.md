<objective>
Finish the Decision Coach (OPT-03, D-04) and the deadline behaviour of D-05 on the API side.

Purpose: the coach today answers one question at a time from the evidence and asks the model to mention the deadline, which it may skip. A stress test is only a sentence the model may or may not add: the learner cannot actually run one. The deadline card and the decision nudge both vanish the moment the deadline passes, although D-05 says the card stays until a choice is made. A failed AI call turns into a 500.

Output: structured deadline and conversation memory on the coach, three runnable stress tests, a graceful "resting" reply, and an overdue-aware decision nudge.

What already exists (do not redo): POST /v1/optionals/coach with grounded evidence (explorer facts plus stored quiz answers), naming-guard filter on output, the model asked for one optional stress test; the decision nudge (kind `decision`, 14 days or fewer, three tones, AI copy with template fallback, 3-per-day cap and 3-hour gap) in apps/api/src/services/coach.ts.

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Severity | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|----------|-----------|-----------------|
| T-07-06 | Tampering | prompt injection through question, history or answer text | medium | mitigate | all learner text is labelled as data in the prompt; output schema is a single string or the existing two fields; length caps (question 500, history 4 x 1700, answer 600) enforced by zod; output filtered with checkText |
| T-07-07 | Information disclosure | coach evidence includes the learner's quiz answers | low | accept | evidence goes only to Vertex AI in asia-south1 under the project's existing AI terms and is keyed to request.uid; no other learner's data is ever in a prompt |
| T-07-08 | Denial of service | repeated stress-test or coach calls driving Vertex spend | medium | mitigate | the hours and regret tests make no AI call; the answer test has a 600-character cap and one model call; per-user cost is logged by the existing ai_calls path against the under-US$2 monthly budget |
| T-07-09 | Spoofing | endpoints accessed without a learner identity | high | mitigate | routes sit under the existing authenticated /v1 scope; every query uses request.uid; tests run with a bearer token only |
| T-07-10 | Repudiation | misleading model advice on the optional choice | low | mitigate | the answer is grounded only in the evidence block, states uncertainty when evidence is missing, and the app shows the facts behind hours verdicts |
