<objective>
Bring the Fit Quiz, results, explorer and compare screens up to the binding UI contract (docs/UI_SPEC.md section 10.11) and to what OPT-01 and OPT-02 ask for.

Purpose: the screens exist but follow neither the contract nor the requirement text: the quiz is a single long form (the contract says one question per screen), results are cards with no fit dots, the explorer is four lines of caption text (no Stat row, no band chart, no "your fit"), compare is a vertical stack of cards rather than side-by-side columns with the best value per row marked, and loading or failure leaves a blank screen. The success-rate band is shown only as numbers.

Output: four small presentational primitives, pure tested helpers, a stepwise quiz with re-openable results, an explorer with the Stat row, band chart and personal fit, and a compare table.

What already exists (do not redo): apps/mobile/src/app/optionals/{index,quiz,[slug],compare,coach}.tsx and the api client methods optionals, optional, compareOptionals, fitQuiz, decision, askCoach, chooseOptional; the Today deadline card and the You row link to /optionals. The coach screen and the explorer's Decide block are replaced in plan 07-05, so leave their behaviour unchanged here (move the Decide block as is if the explorer rewrite needs it).
</objective>
